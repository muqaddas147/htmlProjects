About Our University

Mirpur University of Science and Technology (MUST) is a higher education institution located in Mirpur, Azad Jammu and Kashmir, Pakistan.
The university provides educational opportunities in various academic fields, including engineering, sciences, and technology.
MUST aims to promote learning, research, innovation, and professional growth among students.
